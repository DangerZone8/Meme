Use it in Google Meet, Zoom, Teams & more

  1. Click Start meme camera and allow webcam access in Windows.
  2. In your meeting app, open Settings → Video / Camera.
  3. Select OBS Virtual Camera or the It's Giving virtual camera if it is listed.
  4. In Google Meet, use ⋮ → Settings → Video → Camera.

OBS Studio may be needed if your system does not expose the app's virtual camera. Open OBS and start its Virtual Camera, then select OBS Virtual Camera in the meeting app. The app does not silently activate your camera; you start and stop it here.
